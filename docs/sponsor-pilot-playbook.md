# Circle Panda — Direct Sponsor Pilot Playbook

## What we are selling

Circle Panda can offer brands a clearly labelled, country-targeted community campaign with an agreed delivery window and a report showing recorded impressions and outbound clicks. The first deliverable should be a small pilot, not a promise of mass reach before we have verified audience data.

## The first offer: a two-week sponsored community pilot

- **Placement:** one clearly labelled native sponsored card inside the Circle Panda Home feed.
- **Targeting:** country selection, campaign dates and a destination URL.
- **Creative:** sponsor-approved headline/message, destination and optional offer code.
- **Reporting:** recorded impressions, outbound clicks, click-through rate, dates, targeting and campaign status.
- **User trust:** sponsored material is labelled. Users are never told to click an ordinary ad to earn rewards. Any survey/reward campaign must state the task and reward up front, and reward only a verified completed task—not an ad click.
- **Commercial terms:** agree a fixed pilot fee and delivery cap in writing. Do not sell invoice-grade CPM or conversion guarantees until server-side validation, budget caps, fraud controls and trusted conversion callbacks are in place.

## Pilot success criteria

Agree the exact goal with the sponsor before the campaign begins. Possible goals include visits to a campaign page, offer-code redemptions, opt-in research responses, event registrations or completed sign-ups on the sponsor's own site. Only report the outcome Circle Panda can actually verify.

For survey/research campaigns, show the task and reward before the user starts, collect only necessary data, get consent, and reward a verified completion. Do not reward ad clicks.

## Sales follow-up sequence

1. **Before contact:** make sure signup, moderation/reporting, privacy notice, campaign labelling and analytics work in a production-like test.
2. **Initial approach:** send a short note and one-page pilot concept; ask who owns digital partnerships/media campaigns.
3. **Discovery call:** learn the sponsor's objective, audience, creative approval, budget, measurement needs and compliance requirements.
4. **Written pilot agreement:** define dates, placements, countries, budget, capped delivery, cancellation, reporting, data handling, payment schedule and what counts as success.
5. **Launch:** use approved creative, verify tracking, record baseline and monitor delivery daily.
6. **Close-out:** share a dated report and propose a repeat campaign only after the sponsor reviews the evidence.

## Revenue and trust rules

- Panda Coins (BC) are in-app rewards, not cash. Never describe BC as cash unless a separately funded and legally reviewed cash payout system exists.
- Do not promise user cash earnings before a sponsor-funded task and payout workflow is funded, tested and documented.
- Keep campaign delivery records separate from wallet reward records.
- Client-side impression/click events in the current pilot are useful for QA and indicative reporting, but can be manipulated. They are not yet sufficient as the sole basis for sponsor invoices.
- Do not collect or share personal member data with a sponsor without a clear lawful basis, user notice/consent where required, and appropriate data minimisation.
- Avoid claims about total reach, conversion, demographics or earnings until supported by measured records.
